## Teaching Approach

I view economics as a practical framework for understanding decisions, institutions, and public-policy challenges. My teaching therefore connects economic theory with current issues in food systems, agricultural markets, inequality, and international development.

My approach is guided by three principles:

- **Build intuition before introducing technical detail.** I use accessible examples, graphs, and discussion to establish the economic reasoning behind formal models.
- **Connect concepts with evidence.** Students work with real-world cases and data to understand how economic questions are evaluated empirically.
- **Create an inclusive learning environment.** I encourage questions, provide constructive feedback, and use multiple ways of explaining difficult concepts to support students with different academic backgrounds.

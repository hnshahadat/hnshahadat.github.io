## Teaching Approach

I view economics as a practical framework for understanding individual decisions, markets, institutions, and public-policy challenges. My teaching is guided by three principles:

- **Build intuition first.** I use accessible examples, graphs, and discussion to establish the reasoning behind formal models.
- **Connect concepts with evidence.** I incorporate real-world cases and data to demonstrate how economists evaluate empirical questions.
- **Support an inclusive classroom.** I encourage questions, provide constructive feedback, and explain difficult concepts in multiple ways.
